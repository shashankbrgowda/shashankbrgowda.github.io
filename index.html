About

I'm a software engineer with 10 years of experience, currently at EMBL-EBI in Cambridge, where I build tools that help researchers explore and annotate genomic data.

I'm on the Ensembl team, working mostly on the new website for Ensembl, a genome browser and public database used by researchers worldwide.

I've also worked on Apollo3 (bioRxiv), a genome annotation editor built as a JBrowse 2 plugin. I focused on the features needed by Havana, the EMBL-EBI team that curates GENCODE, the reference gene annotations for human and mouse. I also built a custom plugin for their workflow and a service that syncs their annotations into Otter, their existing annotation system.

Before EMBL-EBI, I worked for a Fortune 50 retailer, designing and building low-latency inventory streaming systems and microservices.

Outside work, I hike, play badminton, and take photos when I travel.
