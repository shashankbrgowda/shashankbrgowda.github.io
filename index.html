About

I'm a software engineer with 10 years of experience, currently working for EMBL-EBI in Cambridge. I build tools and web apps that help researchers annotate and explore genome data. Before this, I worked for a Fortune 50 retailer, where I designed and built low-latency inventory streaming systems and microservices. In my free time I like hiking and playing badminton, and I take pictures whenever I travel.

Work Experience

  • Software Developer, EMBL-EBI 2022 – Present

    Worked on Apollo3 (bioRxiv), a genome annotation plugin for JBrowse 2. Developed features for the Havana team in Apollo3, along with a custom plugin and an integration service that bring annotation data from Apollo3 into our legacy Otter annotation database. Set up the Kubernetes environment and handled deployments of Apollo3 and the integration service for the Havana team. As part of the Ensembl team, worked on various features of the new Ensembl website, and set up monitoring and alerting for its backend services using Prometheus and Grafana.

  • Senior Software Engineer, Lowe's 2020 – 2022

    Redesigned and developed the inventory consumption and management system, working closely with the architect. Moved inventory aggregation from Kafka Streams (RocksDB) to PostgreSQL and replaced the Hazelcast caching layer with Netflix Hollow. Developed the inventory reservation microservice and a low-latency inventory picture microservice on the new caching layer, handling ~15k requests per second with a p99 latency of ~10 ms. Provided on-call support and took part in war rooms during peak sales events.

  • Software Engineer, Lowe's 2018 – 2020

    Developed inventory streaming services with Kafka Streams that consumed and aggregated out-of-order inventory updates from 1,800+ stores and 200+ other Lowe's facilities, with snapshots stored in Cassandra as the single source of truth across omnichannel platforms. At peak, during nightly batch updates, these services handled 20k–40k events per second. Also worked on features in the inventory pickup microservice and the Hazelcast caching service.
    Worked with the performance engineering team to load-test the inventory services and tuned them to improve performance. Provided on-call support and took part in war rooms during peak sales events.

  • Associate Software Engineer, Lowe's 2016 – 2018

    As part of the import sourcing team, worked on a commercial and service invoice validation web application that automatically flagged mismatches between invoices and purchase orders for business users. Also worked on a port allocation web application that let business users route shipping containers to different Lowe's facilities based on demand.

  • System Software Engineer Intern, Akamai Technologies 2016

    Worked on Pliny, Akamai's internal centralized log analysis tool built with ELK. Wrote scripts to ingest access logs in different formats into Elasticsearch.
